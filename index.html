DeepFake Detection Using MobileNetV2 and LSTM

Co-authored a research paper introducing a DeepFake detection system that processes video frames to identify manipulated faces. The pipeline uses MobileNetV2 for spatial feature extraction, LSTM for temporal analysis, and MTCNN for face detection. Through iterative threshold optimization, we improved model accuracy from 85.71% to 95% on the Celeb-DF benchmark dataset — effectively balancing the trade-off between false positives and false negatives while preserving high accuracy on natural videos.
